🌍 Wild Worlds: Become a Little Explorer

Wild Worlds is an interactive, educational platform game designed for children aged 5–8. Built with Phaser.js, the game encourages players to explore different habitats, discover and collect animals, and learn about where they live, what they eat, and how they adapt to their environments.

🎮 How to Play

1. Choose a World

From the main menu, select a habitat to explore: Safari, Aviary, or Ocean.

2. Match the Animal

Choose an animal from the bottom of the screen and drag it to the correct habitat. Think carefully about where each animal belongs!

3. Match the Food

Once you have matched the animal to its habitat, you must match it with its correct diet. When you make the right match, you’ll unlock an educational animal card to learn more about it.

4. Complete the Quiz

Ready for a challenge? Select Quiz Time! from the menu and answer the animal questions. Choose the answer you think is correct and see how many stars you can earn based on what you've learned.

5. Collect Stickers

As you play and make correct matches, you will unlock animal stickers. Visit the Sticker Book from the main menu to see all the animals you have collected across every habitat.

🏆 Your Goal

Explore all the habitats, learn about the animals, answer the quiz questions, and collect all 30 animal stickers!

💡 Explorer Tips

Read carefully: The animal facts on the cards will help you ace the quiz!

Observe: Look closely at each animal and its habitat for clues.

Keep trying: If you make a mistake matching an animal or its food, don't worry—just try again!

Be thorough: Explore every single world to complete your Sticker Book.

Have fun exploring the Wild World!
